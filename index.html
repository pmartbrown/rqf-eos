01

Core Values

Authentic, not aspirational — these describe who RQF already is at its best. We hire, fire, reward, and recognize based on these five values.

01
Immediate Firing Offense

Integrity

Ethical conduct in every transaction — including protecting parties we are not obligated to protect. Zero tolerance for internal dishonesty or cover-ups.

The only hard-line immediate termination standard. All other values: coach first. This one: no second chance.

02

Stewardship

Responsible management of capital, relationships, and business operations. RQF operates as a fiduciary in practice — for fund investors, for clients' deals, and for internal records.

Transparency earns trust. Trust earns more capital access.

03

Diligence

Rigorous adherence to process and fiduciary responsibility. Proactive vigilance that supersedes process — you don't wait for a checklist to tell you something is wrong.

Born from experience: missing steps cost the company ~$50K on a deal with forged signatures. This value exists because of that.

04

Urgency

Every deal is treated like it can't wait. Speed and availability are both expressions of this value. If someone can't develop urgency, it is ultimately a fireable offense.

Distinction: Speed = table stakes (Three Uniques). Urgency = the internal disposition that produces speed. Two different things.

05

Extra Mile (working label)

You see something and you act — for a teammate, a client, a competitor, the community — without being asked. Goes beyond what's required in every direction.

Label may still evolve. Closest character references: Good Samaritan, Sam Gamgee (LOTR). "Go-Giver" resonates but is overused in this ecosystem.
