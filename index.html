01

Core Values

Authentic, not aspirational — these describe who RQF already is at its best. We hire, fire, reward, and recognize based on these five values.

01
Stewardship
Responsible management of capital, relationships, and business operations. RQF operates as a fiduciary in practice — for fund investors, for clients' deals, and for internal records.
Transparency earns trust. Trust earns more capital access.
02
Urgency
Every deal is treated like it can't wait. Speed and availability are both expressions of this value. If someone can't develop urgency, it is ultimately a fireable offense.
Distinction: Speed = table stakes (Three Uniques). Urgency = the internal disposition that produces speed.
03
Diligence
Rigorous adherence to process and fiduciary responsibility. Proactive vigilance that supersedes process — you don't wait for a checklist to tell you something is wrong.
Born from experience: missing steps cost the company ~$50K on a deal with forged signatures.
04
Integrity
Immediate Firing Offense
Ethical conduct in every transaction — including protecting parties we're not obligated to protect. Zero tolerance for internal dishonesty or cover-ups.
The only hard-line immediate termination standard. All other values: coach first. This one: no second chance.
05
Extra Mile
working label
You see something and you act — for a teammate, a client, a competitor, the community — without being asked. Goes beyond what's required in every direction.
Label may still evolve. Closest references: Good Samaritan, Sam Gamgee (LOTR). "Go-Giver" resonates but is overused in the ecosystem.

Marketing Strategy

Four components. Each one should be tight and specific. This is how RQF wins its ideal clients and turns away the wrong ones.

⚡
Working Draft — Drafted from session context. Needs Paul + Justin review. Speed as a Unique is intentional — it's table stakes externally even though it's not a Core Value internally.
1 Target Market — The List

Creative real estate investors who are already fluent in non-traditional financing — specifically active Gator method operators, subject-to and seller finance buyers, double-close wholesalers, and Morby/Stack/Echo method practitioners. Primary fishing pond: Pace Morby's Gator community, Sub2 Owners Club, Owner's Club, and local creative finance networks across the US.

These investors already understand the deal structure. RQF doesn't educate them on how creative finance works — we fund it. The secondary market is capital investors: high-net-worth individuals seeking 12%+ annual returns at bond-level risk through the fund.

2 Three Uniques
1
Speed that doesn't sleep
Same-day decisions. Next-day wires. Every deal is treated like it closes tomorrow — because it probably does. Our response time is a competitive advantage for every borrower we work with.
2
We speak your language
The only transactional funder fluent in Gator, Sub2, Morby, echo, and stack method deals. You don't have to explain the structure or educate the capital source. We already know. We just fund it.
3
Relationship capital
You get Paul or Justin on the phone. Not a call center, not a portal, not a "submit and wait." RQF invests in the relationship across every deal — our borrowers become long-term partners, not one-off transactions.
3 Proven Process — The RQF Bridge
1
Connect
Reach out through community, referral, or direct. 15-minute call to understand the deal and structure.
2
Qualify
Deal structure review: escrow security, timeline, deal type, amounts. Decision made same day.
3
Commit
Wire details confirmed, docs aligned with title and escrow. Paul or Justin signs off personally.
4
Fund
Capital deployed into escrow. Deal can close. Borrower has what they need.
5
Close & Return
Deal executes. Capital returns to RQF. Relationship deepens — next deal conversation starts here.
"The RQF Bridge" ties directly to the Core Focus bridge metaphor. Alternative: "The Fast Bridge." Confirm the name with Justin.
4 Guarantee
"If your deal is in escrow and the structure qualifies — we fund it, or we tell you why it doesn't within 2 hours. No ghosting. No runaround. No surprises at the wire."
This makes RQF's implicit promise explicit. "No ghosting" is a known frustration in the Gator community — naming it is a direct competitive shot. The 2-hour standard creates an internal accountability bar.
